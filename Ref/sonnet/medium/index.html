03 — WHAT IT DOES

Three primitives. No dashboard sprawl.

CORRELATE

One timeline, all telemetry

Metrics, logs and traces land on a shared clock. Click a latency spike and the exact log lines and downstream spans are already selected — no tab-switching, no query language to memorise.

DETECT

Baselines that learn your traffic

Nordveil models each service's normal shape per hour and per weekday, then alerts on deviation — not on a static threshold someone set in 2021 and forgot.

ROUTE

Ownership-aware routing

Every alert carries the owning team, the last deploy, and a suggested runbook. It reaches the person who can fix it — and skips everyone who can't.

04 — HOW IT WORKS

From zero to signal in one afternoon.

STEP 01

Point an agent at your cluster

One Helm chart or a single binary. It auto-discovers services and starts streaming within minutes — no per-service instrumentation required to begin.

STEP 02

Let baselines settle

Over the first 24–48 hours Nordveil learns each service's rhythm. You watch the instrument fill in; nothing pages yet.

STEP 03

Wire routing & go live

Map teams to services, connect Slack or PagerDuty, and flip detection on. The next real incident arrives pre-triaged.
