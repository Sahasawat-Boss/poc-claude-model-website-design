The platform

One layer
over everything you run.

Most tools give you dashboards. Nordveil gives you a verdict — the specific service, the specific deploy, the blast radius — computed the moment a signal drifts. Built for teams without a platform division.

[ 01 ]

Deploy-aware correlation

Every metric anomaly is automatically checked against your deploy timeline. If latency climbed 40 seconds after a ship, that commit is named in the alert — not left for you to bisect at 3 a.m.

[ 02 ]

Alerts that earn the interrupt

Adaptive baselines learn each service's normal shape per hour and per weekday. Seasonal traffic stops paging you. Real regressions still do — with a confidence score and the three most-correlated signals attached.

Traces without the sampling tax

Tail-based sampling keeps 100% of slow and errored requests and a smart slice of the rest. You get the outlier trace that actually explains the incident, at a fraction of the storage bill.

How it works

From zero to
correlated in one afternoon.

No agent fleet to manage, no query language to learn before you get value. Point Nordveil at your telemetry and it starts building the model.

Step 01

Send telemetry

Drop in the OpenTelemetry endpoint or install the single collector. Prometheus and StatsD scrape targets work unchanged.

nordveil connect --otlp
Step 02

Link your repo

Authorize the GitHub or GitLab app. Deploy markers and commit metadata flow in automatically from CI.

nordveil link github
Step 03

Baselines build

Within ~6 hours the model has learned each service's rhythm. Alerts stay muted until baselines are confident.

nordveil status --baselines
Step 04

Get the verdict

When something drifts, you get one notification naming the service, the likely cause, and the blast radius.

nordveil incidents --open
