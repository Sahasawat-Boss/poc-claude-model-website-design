What it does

Three things, done without ceremony.

No plugin marketplace to assemble. No week of YAML. The parts that matter, wired on install.

F.01

Correlated tracing

Every request stitched across services, queues and databases — so a spike points at a cause, not a haystack.

F.02

Adaptive thresholds

Baselines learn your traffic shape by hour and weekday. Alerts fire on genuine deviation, not on Monday morning.

F.03

Incident replay

Scrub back to any minute in the last 30 days. Full metric, log and topology state — the way it actually looked.

Getting running

From zero to signal in one afternoon.

Drop in the agent

One binary or a sidecar container. It auto-discovers services and starts reporting in under five minutes.

Let it learn a week

Nordveil watches normal for seven days and builds per-service baselines. You do nothing.

Route the alerts

Point deviations at Slack, PagerDuty or a webhook. Tune the noise floor once and leave it.
